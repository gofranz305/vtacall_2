How VitaCall Works in 4 Steps

1

Submit Dorm Checkup Request

Student selects symptoms, urgency level, temperature, and verifies their Villanueva boarding house room & landmarks.

2

Triage & Nurse Dispatch

Campus health personnel receive instant alerts, accept the assignment, and travel to the student's boarding house with a mobile kit.

3

On-Site Clinical Examination

The nurse examines the student on-site, records blood pressure, temperature, pulse, SpO2, diagnosis, and care instructions.

4

Verifiable Medical Certificate

An official USTP Medical Certificate with QR code and SHA-256 hash is automatically issued to excuse missed academic classes.
